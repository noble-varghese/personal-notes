## ☕ Coffee & Mocha Flavored Shakes

### 1. Vanilla Coffee Shake (Iced Latte Style)
**Best for:** Mornings, refreshing post-workout

**Ingredients:**
- 1 scoop vanilla protein  
- 1 shot espresso or ½ cup strong brewed coffee (chilled)  
- ½ cup cold milk or almond milk  
- Ice cubes  
- Optional: pinch of cinnamon or cocoa powder  

**Instructions:**  
Blend everything for 30 seconds. Add more ice if you want it extra cold and light.

---

### 2. Mocha Shake (Coffee + Cocoa Masker)
**Best for:** Rich flavor that kills the vanilla taste

**Ingredients:**
- 1 scoop vanilla protein  
- ½ cup cold coffee or milk  
- 1 tsp unsweetened cocoa powder  
- 1 tsp instant coffee (if no brewed coffee)  
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup (optional)  
- Ice cubes  

**Instructions:**  
Blend until smooth. You’ll get a mocha-cold-brew feel.

---

### 3. Peanut Butter Cold Brew Shake
**Best for:** Heavier post-workout fuel (light-ish but satisfying)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 scoop vanilla protein  
- ½ cup chilled coffee or almond milk  
- 1 tbsp peanut butter  
- Ice cubes  
- Pinch of sea salt (optional)  

**Instructions:**  
Blend until creamy. Skip sweeteners — the PB and protein will handle it.

---

### 4. Pineapple Ginger Zing (Refreshing Fruity Option)
**Best for:** Hot days, tropical feel

**Ingredients:**
- 1 scoop vanilla protein  
- ½ cup cold water or coconut water  
- ½ cup frozen pineapple  
- ½ tsp grated ginger (fresh or powdered)  
- Ice cubes  

**Instructions:**  
Blend for a citrusy, ginger-spiked, fresh taste.

## 🍌 Chocolate, Banana & Avocado Shakes

### 1. Choco-Avocado Recovery Shake
**Creamy, chocolatey, and nutritious — avocado adds smoothness.**

**Ingredients:**
- 1 scoop vanilla protein  
- ½ ripe avocado  
- 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder  
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup (optional)  
- ½ cup milk (or almond milk)  
- Ice cubes  

**Instructions:**  
Blend until smooth. Add more milk if too thick — it should be creamy but drinkable.

---

### 2. Banana Chocolate Shake (Simple & Safe Bet)
**Classic post-workout shake with chocolate-banana flavor masking vanilla**

**Ingredients:**
- 1 scoop vanilla protein  
- 1 small banana  
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder  
- ½ cup milk or water  
- Ice cubes  

**Instructions:**  
Blend until frothy. Add cinnamon for extra flavor.

---

### 3. Avocado-Banana Smooth Shake (Mild & Light)
**Mild flavor, smooth texture — not overly sweet or rich**

**Ingredients:**
- 1 scoop vanilla protein  
- ½ banana  
- ¼ avocado  
- ½ cup almond milk  
- Ice cubes  
- Optional: pinch of cinnamon or nutmeg  

**Instructions:**  
Blend until creamy. Good for sensitive palates.
